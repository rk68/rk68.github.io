Halo

A local AI assistant with its own desktop.

Halo runs H Company's Holo models on a Mac through llama.cpp and Apple Metal. Holo models are open-weight vision-language models built for computer use. You chat with bots that keep saved memory, and each bot can work in its own sandboxed Linux desktop. Halo stores conversations locally with Convex.

What it does

  • Create named bots with their own instructions, saved memory, avatar colour and style.
  • Keep separate conversation threads that survive an app restart.
  • Download, load, switch and unload local models from the sidebar.
  • Give a bot a disposable desktop with optional web access.
  • Review each desktop action, or turn on automatic approval for one sandbox.
  • Share desktop screenshots in chat and save them as PNG files.
