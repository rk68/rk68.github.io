Holistic AI x UCL hackathon

Third place. Multi-objective optimisation for trustworthy AI in tabular classification.

Our team, Team ARM, took third place in Track 1 of the Holistic AI x UCL hackathon. The track asked teams to train classifiers on tabular data that balance several trustworthiness goals at once, rather than accuracy alone.
