HyPA-RAG

Adaptive retrieval for AI legal and policy questions, built with Holistic AI.

Language models answer legal and policy questions poorly when their knowledge is out of date or the text mixes qualitative rules with numeric thresholds. Retrieval helps, but a fixed retrieval setup either misses context on hard questions or wastes cost on easy ones.

HyPA-RAG, a hybrid parameter-adaptive retrieval-augmented generation system, adapts to each question. We tested it on New York City Local Law 144, which regulates automated hiring tools.

How it works

  • A classifier rates how complex each question is and sets the retrieval parameters to match.
  • Retrieval combines dense search, sparse keyword search and a knowledge graph.
  • An evaluation framework scores answers with set question types and metrics.

On Local Law 144, HyPA-RAG improved correctness, faithfulness and contextual precision.
